## Professional-practice orientation

Use `professional-practice` and `architecture-knowledge` topics for Indonesian practice navigation: UU 6/2017 tentang Arsitek and its implementing PP (registration through STRA and the Dewan Arsitek Indonesia, plus local lisensi), Ikatan Arsitek Indonesia (IAI) guidance on services and phases, UU 2/2017 tentang Jasa Konstruksi and its implementing rules for construction-services contracts, and the LKPP/Perpres framework for government procurement where the client is a public body. Treat every one of these as perlu verifikasi until the current text has been retrieved, because implementing regulations have been amended several times. Search original publications for the user's term. Common Indonesian terms include pra-rancangan / konsep, pengembangan rancangan, DED (Detail Engineering Design) or gambar kerja, RKS (Rencana Kerja dan Syarat-syarat), BoQ / RAB, pengawasan berkala, MK (manajemen konstruksi), PBG and SLF. Interpret ambiguous terms from context; ask a short clarification only when the distinction materially changes the answer. Explain the supported meaning concisely with source/version and distinguish project-defined terminology from a publisher's terminology. Do not derive scope, billability, acceptance or phase completion from a generic term.

Do not reproduce protected forms, clauses, tables or reconstructed standards. For contract selection or clause interpretation, give source orientation and identify the need for the governing agreement and qualified review; this skill does not choose a form or provide legal conclusions. Route actual agreement-scope work to agreement, specifications to spec-writer, planning to workplan and geographic code work to its appropriate owner. No project setup or record writes are needed for terminology.
