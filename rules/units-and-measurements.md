# Units & Measurements

These conventions apply to all skill outputs.

## System

- Default to **SI (metric)** units, as used in Indonesian regulations and SNI standards: metres (m), millimetres (mm) for construction dimensions, square metres (m²), cubic metres (m³), kilograms, °C, millimetres of rainfall.
- Currency defaults to **Rupiah (IDR)**, written `Rp 1.250.000` in Indonesian-language documents or `IDR 1,250,000` in English-language documents. Never mix the two separator styles in one document.
- If a supplied source uses imperial units, convert explicitly and show the conversion once (1 ft = 0.3048 m; 1 ft² = 0.092903 m²); never present an unconverted imperial value as the result.
- Never mix unit systems within a single table, calculation, or paragraph.

## Area

- Label the reported area type explicitly, using the term defined by the governing source, for example **luas lantai bangunan** (total floor area counted for KLB), **luas dasar bangunan** (footprint counted for KDB), **luas lahan** (plot area), gross or net area for occupant-load tables, or a user-defined net/usable area.
- Area labels do not establish measurement boundaries or a conversion formula. Retrieve the applicable original measurement rule (for KLB/KDB, the kabupaten/kota RDTR or bangunan gedung perda; for occupant load, the table's footnotes) or state the user's explicit project method before calculating. Preserve its inclusions, exclusions and boundary; do not infer these from an abbreviation.
- When converting between area types, state the actual source method and factor. Treat an unsourced factor as an explicit design assumption, never a standard requirement.

## Dimensions

- Format: **P × L × T** (panjang × lebar × tinggi) or **L × W × H** in English, separated by ` × ` (space-times-space)
- Always include units: `3.600 mm × 3.000 mm` or `3,60 m × 3,00 m` in Indonesian-language documents; `3.60 m × 3.00 m` in English-language documents
- Drawings and schedules dimension in millimetres unless the project states otherwise; levels in metres with three decimals (`+3.600`)

## Quantities

- Always state the basis: "per lantai", "per orang", "per m²", "per m'"
- Round to appropriate precision: areas to 0.01 m², dimensions to the nearest millimetre, percentages to one decimal
- Indonesian-language documents use `.` for thousands and `,` for decimals (`1.250,50 m²`); English-language documents use `,` for thousands and `.` for decimals (`1,250.50 m²`). Pick one per document.
